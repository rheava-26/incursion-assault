# Incursion Assault — Worldbuilding Canon

*Consolidated 2026-07-03 from the designer's technology/worldbuilding doc, the hand-drawn
Centak Islands map, and the original Canva concept deck. **This doc supersedes the GDD where
they conflict.** Unfinished by design — sections marked (provisional) await designer sign-off.*

---

## 1. The world

A planet slightly smaller than Earth: **~0.85 g**, with a **denser, higher-oxygen atmosphere**.
Consequences the game actually models: shells hang and decelerate ("floaty" ballistics — see
`WORLD`/`GRAVITY`/`AIR_DRAG` in the prototype), fires burn hot, big airborne things are viable.
The planet's **poles are ancient propulsion structures** that originally pushed the planet into
orbit; they remain studded with teleporters that can carry travelers pole-to-pole.

## 2. The setting: the Centak Islands — "The Scramble"

A large fractured archipelago, mineral-rich and strategically placed, holding some of the
world's **largest stockpiles of mana crystals**. Every power wants a piece; the resulting
colonial land-grab is called **The Scramble**.

Map regions (from the designer's sketch):
- **Canis** — northwestern island mass.
- **Serds** — northeastern island mass.
- **Central island** (circled on the map; name TBD) — ringed, likely volcanic; prime
  crystallizer territory.
- **Toles** — small southern island.
- **Southwestern cluster** — outlying chain, unclaimed frontier.
- Centura holds **the eastern island** and several trade ports.

## 3. Mana & magic systems

**Mana crystals** form where ambient mana is trapped by pressure and crystallized over time —
deep underground or in volcanically active zones. Mined via **crystallizers** at key sites.
They are the strategic resource of the setting.

- **Mana engines** — the only practical way to make airships. Provide a protective forcefield
  around airship balloons (the balloons are otherwise fragile; the engines themselves are
  vulnerable). Power all mana-based systems: storage converters, shield barriers, mixed weapon
  systems (multistage magiartillery), blink drives, and — with very large engines —
  **balloonless skyships**. Run essentially forever. Efficiency rule: **a few big engines beat
  many small ones**; more crystals = bigger ships.
- **Inertial dampeners** — large tractor-beam-esque devices with frequency-attuned dampener
  boxes; yank objects to a near-instant stop by sapping acceleration energy and dispersing it
  slowly. Uniform velocity removal (not deceleration). Enable: extremely short/no runways,
  planes staying landed on moving vehicles, mid-air drop-stops, near-vertical climbs (slow).
  Cost: severe range reduction + airspeed limits → used mostly on zeppelin-launched
  short-range fighters/bombers and small fighter platforms.
- **Mana storage converters** — boxes running an active mana current that hold items
  **dematerialized** until needed; pocket-dimension hangars. Need fairly large hangar space;
  ~a dozen aircraft each. Stored aircraft load the mana engines (limits hull
  shielding/strength — carrying capacity is a real tradeoff, not unlimited). Can store crew,
  but only one "soul" active per converter, and converter failure kills them permanently
  *(designer iffy on the crew use — treat as rare/experimental)*.
- **Blink drives** — short-range teleportation via movement magic; mana-engine powered.
- **Psionics** — deep topic, to be expanded later (see Kolden). Psionic soldiers wield
  **psisolid**: reactive-energy barriers thrown as weapons (spear-equivalents up to manifested
  artillery-caliber projectiles) that can be detonated.
- **Necromancy** — true revival costs absurd mana, but pre-infusing a corpse with life energy
  and running it on simple command spells is cheap. See Asortos.
- **Infusions** — over-dense, over-energetic mana becomes *sapient* and absorbs matter into
  the most efficient vessel it can find: a magically-attuned adult being, naturally magical,
  subsisting on mana. Made by shocking/burning big mana crystals (or slurry), usually with
  crystal catalysts; ~30+ infusions per batch; **too many crystals in one batch makes
  dragons**. Very suggestible, basic knowledge, high-powered magic; some get wings/tails;
  they **explode violently on death**. Blue skin = electrically stimulated, red = fire.
- **Mindlink pylons** — Celtaran-pioneered pylons sharing mental state/emotion in a radius via
  natural mana generation: near-perfect coordination, but linked bodies **cannot cast magic at
  all**. Scales up — bigger groups make the semi-hivemind function better. One Celtaran nation
  of several hundred thousand runs entirely on pylons.
- **Eltara essence** — harvested from the imprisoned goddess **Eltara** by the superpower
  Eltaris; infusing a person creates functionally invulnerable, immortal archmages. Hoarded
  even amid Eltaris's civil war. Geopolitical backdrop, not a game system.

## 4. Materials

- **Adamantine** — absorbs and traps heat extremely well; dumps it near-instantly on water
  contact. Overload = violent pressure explosion. Uses: high-power **steam guns** (long-range
  autorifles), mana-engine cooling (needs a liquid dispersal system), ramming weapons.
- **Depthsteel** — insanely durable high-density metal forged under extreme pressure in
  underwater caverns. Extremely resistant to blunt/sharp damage; very rare, very heavy;
  **easy to enchant**.
- Conventional: oak planking, iron plate, steel plate (already in the prototype's deck table).

## 5. Factions of the Scramble (target ≥7)

1. **Hekani** — airship-heavy power running on its own mana-crystal stockpile + massive lava
   fields to mass-produce **infusions** as replacement population. Advanced (proto-**jet**
   aircraft; single-shot heavy cannon armament) but **understaffed**. Exceptionally
   nationalistic yet ethnically diverse. Here to dominate the islands' crystal resources and
   keep their pseudo-monopoly.
2. **Centura** — the world's largest mindlink **hivemind** (several hundred thousand; 230+
   years old). Mercantile megacorp-nation: buys warships and mercenaries rather than building;
   large fleet of planes, small airships, warships, ground vehicles, plus a few VERY big
   bought warships (Hekani×Sortros tech fusion). Near-immune to mental attack (dispersed),
   deeply infiltrated everywhere (spies/assassins), rents out willing bodies on months-long
   contracts. Holds the eastern island + trade ports (notably trading with Ashterrin). Not
   evil; exceptionally calculating. Seeking a permanent, fortifiable home.
3. **Kolden** — the largest revolt in history against Eltaris rule. Stalin-esque
   authoritarian "communists" who incentivize **psionic activation** by ingesting Northern
   Wyrm venom (hallucinations, mental decay, agony; **40%+ mortality**; the unactivated are
   second-class). Doctrine: absurdly powerful single-shot weapons + massive crews (manpower →
   massed magic attacks), because they lack resources. Signature weapon: thrown/detonating
   **psisolid**. Politically isolated, horrific doctrine, well-intentioned-ish. Here to strip
   resources and build a fleet to kill Eltara-essence bearers.
4. **Asortos** — necromancer nation; capital **Ashterrin**, home of the **Necroforge**
   superbuilding, which animates hundreds of dead per year using life energy tithed in
   negligible percentages from the whole population. **Sells the dead as products** — crewing
   without people, piloted by simple command spells. *(Role in the Scramble: vendor +
   opportunist — provisional.)*
5. **Eltaris** — the stagnant superpower: imprisoned the goddess Eltara, harvests her essence,
   blocked innovation to preserve its power, and is now in a gigantic civil war and at war
   with basically everyone. Off-map pressure that explains Kolden, refugees, and the arms
   market. *(Presence on the islands: expeditionary remnants — provisional.)*
6. **(provisional) The Celtaran Concord** — mindlink-pylon homeland (distinct from Centura's
   hivemind); disciplined pylon-network crews, zero shipboard magic. Here to secure crystal
   supply for their pylon grid.
7. **(provisional) The Toles Compact** — native islander league of the southern/SW islands;
   scrappy, defensive, intimate knowledge of the archipelago; salvaged/hybrid tech; the
   "home team" the Scramble is happening *to*.

*(6–7 are placeholders to hit the "at least 7 factions" target — rename/replace freely.)*

## 6. Gameplay hooks the code should honor

- Mana engines: few-big > many-small (efficiency curve); envelope forcefield tied to engine
  survival; engine = high-value target.
- Dampeners trade range/airspeed for runway-free ops → catapult/zeppelin fighter doctrine.
- Storage converters cap carried aircraft via mana-engine load, not a hard number.
- Crew variants as coordination dial: **infusions** (cheap, chaotic, explode) → default
  humans (Zeppelin-Wars chaos) → **undead** (obedient, simple, no morale, no berths) →
  **mindlink** (near-perfect coordination, no magic use).
- Adamantine systems run hot: sustained fire → overload risk → pressure explosion; water
  cooling as the release valve.
- Faction ship doctrine feeds enemy presets: Hekani (airships + jets, elite, few), Kolden
  (huge crews, giant single-shot guns), Centura (bought mixed fleets, coordinated),
  Asortos-crewed hulls (undead, fearless, slow).
- Mana crystals are the economy's keystone resource (mines at volcanic/deep sites → harbors →
  fleets).
